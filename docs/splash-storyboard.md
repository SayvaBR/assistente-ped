- Duração: 4,0 s

- Curva principal: ease-out com squash-and-stretch leve no salto

## Prompt para Seedance 2.5

Create a 4-second vertical 9:16 mobile app splash animation using the supplied professor reference and blue mascot reference. Keep the camera locked in a front-facing portrait composition with no cuts, no zoom, no perspective change, and no new characters. Use a clean pale blue-white background with a soft oval halo and subtle ground shadow.

Animate one continuous sequence: a smiling teacher in a vivid blue jacket rises gently from below the frame and waves hello; he eases back and fades into the background. A small rounded blue mascot then emerges from the lower center, anticipates with a soft squash, springs upward along a short curved arc, stretches slightly at the apex, and lands with a soft squash. The mascot smiles, waves one hand, blinks once, and makes one small secondary bounce before settling centered above empty negative space reserved for the app name and loading bar. Add only two tiny golden sparkles near the final pose. Preserve exact character identity, colors, wardrobe, face proportions, and friendly polished 3D illustration style across the entire shot.

Motion must feel fluid and handcrafted: ease-out entrance, curved path, subtle squash-and-stretch, delayed shadow follow-through, soft landing, no jitter, no morphing, no limb duplication, no text, no watermark, no black background, no random props.

## Negative prompt

No hard cuts, no camera shake, no camera movement, no extra people, no changing clothes, no face redesign, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no duplicated mascot, no photorealism, no dark background, no UI text, no subtitles, no logo generated by the model, no particles beyond two small golden sparkles.
